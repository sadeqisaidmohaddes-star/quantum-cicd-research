## Notes on confidence ratings

- **High confidence** = primary source opened and directly inspected (repo code/CI YAML, full paper text/abstract, or independently-resolved DOI record), with no material ambiguity in interpretation.
- **Medium confidence** = primary source opened, but either (a) the claim required reconstructing candidates without a pre-given reference to confirm/deny against, or (b) the finding is an absence result that a differently-worded search might overturn.
- No claim in this table was rated based on a search-result snippet alone.
